# Pawxis: what to offer prospects

Use this sheet when you talk to solo mobile groomers.
**Only say what is on this page.** Every claim here is true in the product today.
If a groomer asks for something that is not here, say "that is coming soon" or "not yet".
Do not promise a date.

Related: `docs/outreach.md` (demo links and the Facebook group post),
`docs/PAWXIS_PRODUCT_GUIDE.md` (every feature in detail).

---

## 1. Who to contact first

**Best fit now**
- Solo groomers with one van.
- They take bookings by text, Instagram or Facebook messages, a paper book, or Google Calendar.
- They use no booking software yet.

Why they fit: there is no client list to move. They already text from their own phone, and Pawxis lets them do that in one tap.

**Harder right now**
- Groomers who use another booking app and have a big client list. There is no import yet.
- Groomers who say automatic texts are a must.

**Not a fit**
- Salons with staff. Fleets with many vans. Pawxis is for one groomer and one van.

## 2. The offer

1. **14 days free.** No card. During the trial they get everything in Pro.
2. **Founding price for the first 20 groomers:** Solo $19 a month or Pro $39 a month, locked for life.
   (Normal price: Solo $29, Pro $59. Yearly = 2 months free.)
   Check the discount works before you promise it. See section 8.
3. **I set it up with you.** About 20 minutes on a call or chat: services, prices, hours, service area, deposit.
4. **Their money stays theirs.** Deposits go straight to their own Stripe account, or to their own payment link. Pawxis takes 0% of deposits. (Stripe's normal card fee still applies.)
5. **No contract.** Month to month. Cancel any time.
6. **They shape the product.** Founding members tell me what to build first.

---

## 3. What they get today

**On both plans (Solo and Pro)**
- A booking page with their name and logo: `pawxis.app/book/theirname`. Clients need no app.
- Deposits up front, so a no-show costs the client, not the groomer.
- A price range shown before the client books, from the groomer's own coat and weight rules.
- A service area (radius or a boundary drawn on a map). Addresses outside it cannot book.
- Weekly hours and days off. A slot is held while a client checks out, so nobody double-books.
- A calendar (day and week, drag to move) and a feed for their phone calendar.
- Digital Pet Cards: coat, temperament and past styles, with a QR code and PDF.
- An invoice (PDF) for every booking.
- Confirmations and reminders **by email**, in the groomer's name. Replies go to the groomer's email.
  The emails have Call, Text and WhatsApp links to the groomer, if they saved a phone number.
- An inbox. The groomer types a message and sends it in one tap by WhatsApp, from their own phone's text app, or by email.
- Business numbers: bookings, deposits, estimated revenue.
- Email allowance: 800 a month on Solo, 2,000 on Pro. Top up 1,000 for $5.

**Pro only**
- **Order Radar:** ranks each new booking by how much extra driving it adds.
- **Fill My Day:** offers a cancelled slot to clients by email.
- **Live "on my way" link:** the client sees the van on a map.
- **Rebooking autopilot:** emails clients when it is time to rebook.
- **Before/after share cards.**

## 4. Not ready yet

Say **"coming soon"** for these four:
- Automatic text messages (SMS)
- Your own domain (`yourname.com`)
- Importing a client list
- A data export button

Say **"not available"** for these. Do not mention a date:
- A website builder or brand colours (only name and logo)
- Google Calendar sync (they get a read-only feed instead)
- Approving each booking before it is confirmed
- Automatic review requests, custom message templates
- Staff accounts, many vans, a client app

*Why no automatic texts (for you, not for prospects):* US phone carriers need every business that sends texts to be registered, and approval takes weeks. You decided to wait until you have a company. Do not explain this unless they ask.

## 5. Honest answers

**Do you text my clients?**
"Not automatically yet. That is coming soon. Today clients get confirmations and reminders by email in your name. From your inbox you can message any client in one tap by WhatsApp or from your own phone's text app."

**Can I use my own website or domain?**
"Not yet. Your page is pawxis.app/book/yourname with your name and logo. Your own domain is coming soon. Put the link in your Instagram bio, your Facebook page, Google Business and on your van."

**Can I bring my clients over?**
"Not yet. Import is coming soon. Clients are added the first time they book. Send your regulars your link from your own phone and ask them to rebook."

**Why you and not a big app?**
"The big apps are bigger and some have automatic texts. Pawxis is only for one groomer with one van. Pro shows how much extra driving each new booking adds. Pawxis takes 0% of your deposits. And you can talk to me directly."

**What does it cost?**
"14 days free, no card. Then $29 a month (Solo) or $59 (Pro). The first 20 groomers pay $19 or $39 for as long as they stay."

**What if I do not pay after the trial?**
"Your page stops taking new bookings. You can still see your data. Pick a plan and it switches back on."

**How do deposits work?**
"You connect your own Stripe account and clients pay by card. The money goes straight to you. If you do not use Stripe, add your own payment link. The client books first and pays through your link, and you mark the deposit as received."

**Do my clients need an app?**
"No. They open a web link on their phone."

**Is it safe? Who are you?**
"I am {yourName}, a developer building this for groomers. Passwords are hashed. The site uses HTTPS. Your Stripe key is stored encrypted. Card numbers never touch Pawxis. If anything is wrong, you reach me directly."
(Do not say "bank-level security", "GDPR compliant" or similar. It is not checked.)

**Does it work outside the US?**
"I built it for US groomers (US addresses, US dollars). I have not tested other countries."
(Say this only if it is true for you. The forms and prices are US style.)

## 7. Rules for contacting people

- **No cold text messages** to prospects. Use email or Instagram and Facebook messages.
- **Cold email in the US needs your real mailing address and a way to opt out** (CAN-SPAM). A P.O. box or mailbox address is fine. Stop writing when someone says stop. (I am not a lawyer. Check this with one.)
- **Follow each Facebook group's rules.** Many only allow promotion in a set thread.
- **One follow-up only.**
- **Never invent numbers, reviews or customers.**
- **Do not give prospects the demo account login.** It is a real account. Use the tour and the sample pages.

## 8. Before you send the first message

These can break things if you skip them.

1. **Vercel: set `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL` to `https://www.pawxis.app` and redeploy.**
   The old value redirects, and Stripe does not follow redirects. Deposit confirmations would not arrive.
   After this, each groomer who already connected Stripe must reconnect their key.
2. **Resend:** check that the sending domain is verified and `BUSINESS_EMAIL` is a real address on it.
   The free plan allows 100 emails a day. Upgrade before you have many groomers.
3. **Reminder schedule.** `vercel.json` runs the reminder job once a day. It needs to run every 15 minutes. With one run a day, reminders go out at odd times (sometimes only an hour before the visit), and same-day bookings get none. Two ways to fix it:
   - Upgrade Vercel to Pro, then ask me to change the schedule.
   - Or use the free site cron-job.org: call `https://www.pawxis.app/api/cron/reminders` every 15 minutes with the header `Authorization: Bearer <your CRON_SECRET>`.
4. **Try it yourself once**, in a private window, with a new email: register, verify email, finish onboarding, make a test booking, open the emails.
5. **Founding price.** In Polar, check the discount: $19 and $39, "forever", at most 20 uses. Do one test checkout.
6. **Legal pages.** They still show `[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]`, `[COMPANY ADDRESS]`, `[CONTACT EMAIL]`, `[JURISDICTION]` and `[EFFECTIVE DATE]`. Send me the real values and I fill them in. Have a lawyer read them before you charge anyone.

**Before you take money**
7. Upgrade Vercel to Pro. Hobby is for non-commercial use only.
8. Set `ADMIN_EMAIL` so you get alerts.
9. Check the Polar message top-up product exists ($5 for 1,000 messages).

## 9. What to learn from the first 10 groomers

Write the answers down. They decide what you build next.

1. How do you remind clients today?
2. Would you pay more for automatic texts? How much?
3. Do you have a client list you need to bring over? How many clients?
4. What is the most annoying part of your day?
5. What would make you switch today?

Keep a simple sheet with these columns: name, city, where you found them, date, what you sent, their answer, next step.
